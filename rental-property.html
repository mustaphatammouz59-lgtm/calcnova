Understanding Rental Property Metrics

Investors use specific metrics to evaluate rental properties. NOI is the income left after operating expenses (but before mortgage). Cap Rate measures the rate of return on the property based on its purchase price. Cash on Cash Return measures the annual return on the actual cash you invested (down payment).
