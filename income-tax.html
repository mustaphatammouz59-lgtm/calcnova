How Income Tax is Calculated

The U.S. uses a progressive tax system, meaning your income is divided into "brackets." You don't pay a single tax rate on all your income; instead, different portions of your income are taxed at increasing rates. This calculator uses the 2024 federal tax brackets (simplified) and assumes the standard deduction.
