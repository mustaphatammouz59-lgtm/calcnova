Why Expense Ratios Matter

Mutual funds charge an annual fee called the "expense ratio" to cover management and administrative costs. Even a seemingly small fee (like 1.5%) can significantly eat into your returns over a long period. This calculator subtracts the expense ratio from your expected annual return to give you a more realistic "net return" figure.
