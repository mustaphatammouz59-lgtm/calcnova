How Much Rent Can I Afford?

Financial experts often recommend the 30% rule, which suggests you should spend no more than 30% of your gross monthly income on rent. However, if you have other debts, you should ensure your total debt (including rent) does not exceed 36% of your gross income. This calculator helps you find a safe rent range.
