The Power of Employer Match

A 401(k) is a retirement savings plan sponsored by an employer. One of the biggest benefits is the "employer match," where your employer contributes a percentage of your salary to your account, up to a certain limit, as long as you contribute too. This is essentially free money. This calculator assumes the employer matches your contribution up to the percentage you enter.
