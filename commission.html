How Commission Works

A commission is a sum of money paid to an employee upon completion of a task, usually selling a certain amount of goods or services. It is often calculated as a percentage of the total sale value. This calculator can help salespeople determine their earnings or help businesses figure out what price they need to sell at to achieve a specific commission goal.
