How Leasing Works

When you lease an asset, you are essentially paying for the depreciation that occurs during the time you use it, plus interest (often called the "rent charge" or "money factor"). Leasing often results in lower monthly payments than buying, but you do not own the asset at the end of the term unless you choose to buy it out for the residual value.
