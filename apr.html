What is APR?

The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) is the true cost of borrowing money. Unlike the simple interest rate, the APR includes the interest rate plus any upfront fees, closing costs, or mortgage points. It provides a more accurate picture of the total cost of the loan, making it easier to compare different loan offers.
