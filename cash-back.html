Which Deal is Better?

Car dealerships often offer a choice: a cash rebate (which lowers the price) with a standard interest rate, or a promotional low interest rate (like 0% or 1.9%) with no rebate. This calculator compares the total cost of both options over the life of the loan to help you decide which one saves you more money.
