What is Depreciation?

Depreciation is an accounting method of allocating the cost of a tangible asset over its useful life. Businesses depreciate long-term assets for both tax and accounting purposes. The Straight Line method spreads the cost evenly over the years, while the Double Declining method depreciates more heavily in the early years.
