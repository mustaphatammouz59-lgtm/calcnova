Arithmetic vs. Geometric Mean

The Arithmetic Mean is simply the average of your returns (add them all up and divide by the number of years). However, this can be misleading for investments. The Geometric Mean (also known as the Compound Annual Growth Rate or CAGR) reflects the actual compound growth of your money over time, accounting for volatility. Investors should always look at the Geometric Mean to understand true performance.
