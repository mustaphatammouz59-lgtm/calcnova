The Rent vs. Buy Dilemma

Deciding whether to rent or buy a home is a major financial decision. Buying builds equity and protects against rising rents, but requires a large upfront down payment and maintenance costs. Renting offers flexibility and lower upfront costs, but builds no equity. This calculator uses a simplified model to compare the costs of both scenarios over time.
