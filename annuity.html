What is an Annuity?

An annuity is a series of equal payments made at regular intervals. This calculator focuses on an "Ordinary Annuity," where payments are made at the end of each period. It calculates the Future Value, which is the total value of all the payments plus the compound interest earned on them by the end of the term.
