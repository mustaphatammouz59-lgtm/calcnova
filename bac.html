Only time sobers you up

The liver clears alcohol at a roughly constant ~0.015% per hour. Coffee, cold air, food, and exercise do not change that rate — they can make you feel more awake while your BAC, and your impairment, stay exactly where they were.

What BAC means

Blood alcohol concentration (BAC) is the standard measure of alcohol intoxication. It is expressed as a percentage of alcohol per volume of blood: in the United States, a BAC of 0.08 (0.08%) means 0.08 grams of alcohol per 100 mL of blood. In the US, the legal driving limit is 0.08% for drivers over 21, the legal drinking age; drivers under 21 face stricter limits that vary by state, from about 0.01% to 0.05%.

Alcohol and health

Alcohol's short-term effects include dehydration and intoxication; longer-term effects can include changes in the metabolism of the liver and brain, and alcoholism — alcohol use that causes problems with mental or physical health. Some studies associate a glass of red wine a day with potential benefits, but those claims have not been rigorously proven, and more recent work recommends that alcohol not be consumed for its potential health benefits. Potential benefits reported for light-to-moderate drinking (about one drink a day: 12 fl oz of beer, 5 fl oz of wine, or 1.5 fl oz of distilled spirits) include reduced risk of heart disease, ischemic stroke, and diabetes — but even light drinking can raise the risk of certain cancers, and the benefits, where they exist, are small next to the risks.

Heavy drinking — more than three drinks a day for women and men over 65, or more than four drinks a day (or 14 a week) for younger men, in the Mayo Clinic's definition — has no health benefits and is linked to certain cancers, sudden death from pre-existing cardiovascular disease, heart-muscle damage and heart failure, stroke, high blood pressure, liver disease, accidental injury or death, and brain damage. Nobody should start drinking for health benefits; anyone who drinks should discuss with their doctor what is safe for them.

Effects by blood alcohol concentration
