How to Evaluate a Real Estate Investment

When investing in real estate, two of the most important metrics are Cash Flow and Cash on Cash Return. Cash flow is the money left over after paying all expenses and the mortgage. Cash on Cash Return measures the annual return on the actual cash you invested (down payment + closing costs).
