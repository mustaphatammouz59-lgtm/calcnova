What is Inflation?

Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services is rising, and subsequently, purchasing power is falling. This calculator shows you two things: 1) How much money you will need in the future to buy the same things you can buy today with your initial amount. 2) What the purchasing power of your current money will be in the future.
