The 50/30/20 Rule

A popular budgeting guideline is the 50/30/20 rule. It suggests you should spend 50% of your net income on needs (housing, food, utilities), 30% on wants (entertainment, dining out), and save the remaining 20% for savings and debt repayment. This calculator helps you see where your money is going.
