Traditional IRA vs. Roth IRA

A Traditional IRA allows you to contribute pre-tax dollars. This means your contributions may be tax-deductible now, lowering your current tax bill. However, when you withdraw the money in retirement, it will be taxed as ordinary income. This is the opposite of a Roth IRA, where you pay taxes now and withdraw tax-free later. This calculator assumes annual contributions.
