About CalcNova

CalcNova's sole focus is to provide fast, comprehensive, convenient, free online calculators. We currently offer 71 calculators in finance — mortgages and real estate, loans and debt, investing, retirement, taxes and income — each built and tested individually, each with the in-depth explanation its topic deserves, and we are still developing more. Our goal is to become the one-stop, go-to site for people who need to make quick calculations, and we believe the internet should be a source of free information: every tool here is completely free, with no registration required.

Every calculator was coded and put through comprehensive testing against published reference results. If you notice even the slightest error, your input is extremely valuable to us — accuracy is the whole point. While most CalcNova calculators are designed to be universally applicable for worldwide usage, some are for specific countries only; for example, the Income Tax Calculator is for United States residents only.
