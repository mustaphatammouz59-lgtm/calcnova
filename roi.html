Understanding ROI

Return on Investment (ROI) is a performance measure used to evaluate the efficiency or profitability of an investment. It is calculated by dividing the net profit (or loss) of an investment by its initial cost. A positive ROI means the investment was profitable, while a negative ROI means it resulted in a loss.
