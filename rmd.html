What is an RMD?

A Required Minimum Distribution (RMD) is the minimum amount of money you must withdraw from your retirement accounts (like a Traditional IRA or 401k) each year once you reach a certain age. Under the SECURE 2.0 Act, the age to start taking RMDs is 73 (or 75 if you were born in 1960 or later). The IRS calculates the RMD by dividing your account balance by a "life expectancy factor." Failing to take the RMD results in a hefty penalty.
