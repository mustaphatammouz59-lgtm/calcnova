What is Present Value?

Present Value (PV) is the current value of a future sum of money or stream of cash flows given a specified rate of return. Future cash flows are discounted at the discount rate, and the higher the discount rate, the lower the present value of the future cash flows. It answers the question: "Would you rather have $10,000 today or $15,000 in 5 years?"
