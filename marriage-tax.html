The "Marriage Penalty" vs "Marriage Bonus"

Depending on how much each spouse earns, getting married can either lower or raise your total income tax bill. A Marriage Bonus occurs when the combined tax bill is lower than what you would pay if you were single. A Marriage Penalty occurs when the combined bill is higher. This usually happens when both spouses have high, relatively equal incomes.
