Mortgage Calculator

Our Mortgage Calculator helps you estimate your monthly home loan payment, total interest, and the overall cost of your mortgage. Enter your home price, down payment, loan term, and interest rate to get an accurate estimate.

How to Use the Mortgage Calculator

  • Enter the purchase price of the home.
  • Add your down payment amount or percentage.
  • Select your loan term (for example, 15 or 30 years).
  • Enter the expected interest rate.
  • Click Calculate to view your estimated monthly payment.

How Mortgage Payments Are Calculated

Mortgage payments are calculated based on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment period. The calculator uses the standard mortgage payment formula to estimate principal and interest payments.

Mortgage Calculator FAQ

What is a mortgage calculator?

A mortgage calculator is a tool that estimates your monthly home loan payments and helps you understand your borrowing costs.

What affects my mortgage payment?

Your payment depends on the home price, down payment, interest rate, loan term, taxes, and insurance costs.

Is a larger down payment better?

A larger down payment can reduce your loan amount and may lower your monthly mortgage payment.
