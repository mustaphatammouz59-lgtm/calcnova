What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a type of loan used to purchase or maintain a home, land, or other types of real estate. The borrower agrees to pay the lender over time, typically in a series of regular payments that are divided into two parts: principal and interest. The property itself serves as collateral to secure the loan.

How to Calculate Mortgage Payments

The monthly payment for a fixed-rate mortgage is calculated using a specific formula that takes into account the loan amount, the interest rate, and the loan term. This calculator uses the standard amortization formula to give you an accurate estimate: M = P [ i(1 + i)^n ] / [ (1 + i)^n – 1 ].

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)?

PMI is usually required if your down payment is less than 20% of the home's value. It protects the lender in case you default on the loan.

Can I pay off my mortgage early?

Yes, many lenders allow extra payments towards the principal. This reduces your total interest and shortens the loan term.
