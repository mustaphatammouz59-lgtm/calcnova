Should You Refinance?

Refinancing can lower your monthly payment or shorten your loan term, but it comes with costs. The "break-even point" is the number of months it takes for your monthly savings to cover the cost of the new loan's closing fees. If you plan to stay in the home longer than the break-even point, refinancing might be a good idea.
