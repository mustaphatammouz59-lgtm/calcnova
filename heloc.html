What is a HELOC?

A Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC) is a revolving line of credit secured by your home. It works similarly to a credit card. It has a "draw period" (usually 10 years) where you can borrow money, and a "repayment period" (usually 20 years) where you must pay back the principal and interest. During the draw period, you are often only required to pay the interest.
