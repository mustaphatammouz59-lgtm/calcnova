Understanding the Estate Tax

The federal estate tax is a tax on your right to transfer property at your death. It consists of an accounting of everything you own or have certain interests in at the date of death. However, the U.S. has a very high exemption limit (for 2024, it's $13.61 million per individual). This means the first $13.61 million of your estate is completely tax-free. Only the amount exceeding this limit is subject to the 40% federal estate tax rate.
