Where Does Your Money Go?

Your gross salary is not what you take home. Before you receive your paycheck, your employer withholds money for federal income taxes, Social Security, and Medicare (FICA taxes). This calculator provides a simplified estimate of these deductions for a standard W-2 employee. Note that state taxes are not included in this simplified version.
