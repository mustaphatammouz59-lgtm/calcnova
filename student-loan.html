Understanding Student Loans

Student loans are designed to help students pay for post-secondary education and the associated fees, such as tuition, books, and living expenses. Repayment usually begins after a grace period (often 6 months) following graduation. This calculator helps you estimate your monthly payments and the total interest you will pay over the life of the loan.
