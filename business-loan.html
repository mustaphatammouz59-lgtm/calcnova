Understanding Business Loans

A business loan can help you expand operations, purchase equipment, or manage cash flow. Unlike personal loans, business loans often come with origination fees. This fee is usually deducted from the loan amount upfront, meaning you receive less than the total principal, but you still pay interest on the full principal amount.
