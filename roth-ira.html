Why Choose a Roth IRA?

A Roth IRA is a retirement account funded with after-tax dollars. This means you pay taxes on the money before you put it in. The major benefit is that when you withdraw the money in retirement (after age 59½ and holding the account for 5 years), your withdrawals are completely tax-free, including all the earnings. This calculator uses annual contributions to estimate your final balance.
