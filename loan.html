What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator is a tool that helps you determine the monthly payments, total interest, and total cost of a loan. Whether you are taking out a personal loan, an auto loan, or a student loan, understanding the financial commitment is crucial before signing any agreement.

How to Use This Loan Calculator

Simply enter the total loan amount you need, the annual interest rate offered by your lender, and the term of the loan in months. The calculator will instantly compute your fixed monthly payment using the standard amortization formula.
