How Debt Consolidation Works

Debt consolidation involves taking out a new loan to pay off multiple high-interest debts (like credit cards). The goal is to get a lower interest rate and combine all your payments into one manageable monthly payment. This calculator compares your current debt scenario with a potential new consolidation loan to show you if it saves you money.
