Why is the Down Payment Important?

The down payment is the portion of the home's purchase price you pay upfront. A higher down payment reduces the loan amount, which means you will pay less interest over time. Additionally, in the U.S., putting down at least 20% usually allows you to avoid paying Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI), which can save you hundreds of dollars a month.
