What is a CD?

A Certificate of Deposit (CD) is a savings account with a fixed interest rate and a fixed withdrawal date (maturity date). In exchange for leaving your money in the account for a specific period, the bank pays you a higher interest rate than a regular savings account. However, withdrawing money before the maturity date usually results in a penalty fee.
