How Much House Can I Afford?

Lenders typically use the 28/36 rule to determine how much you can borrow. This means your monthly housing costs should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income, and your total debt (including the new mortgage) should not exceed 36%. This calculator uses these guidelines to estimate your affordable home price.
