Why Calculate Retirement Savings?

Planning for retirement is one of the most important financial steps you can take. This calculator estimates how much you will have saved by the time you retire, taking into account your current savings, monthly contributions, and expected investment returns. It also factors in inflation to show you the "real" purchasing power of your future savings.
