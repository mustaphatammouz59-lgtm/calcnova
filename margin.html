Margin vs. Markup

It's important to understand the difference between margin and markup. Margin is the ratio of profit to revenue (Sales Price - Cost / Sales Price). Markup is the ratio of profit to cost (Sales Price - Cost / Cost). A 50% margin is equivalent to a 100% markup. This calculator provides both to give you a complete financial picture.
