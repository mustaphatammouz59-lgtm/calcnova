Understanding Future Value

Future Value (FV) is the value of a current asset at a future date based on an assumed rate of growth. The future value is important to investors and financial planners, as they use it to estimate how much an investment made today will be worth in the future. Knowing the future value allows investors to make sound investment decisions based on their anticipated needs.
