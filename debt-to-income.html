What is Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio?

Your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio is the percentage of your gross monthly income that goes toward paying your monthly debts. Lenders use this ratio to determine your ability to manage monthly payments and repay borrowed money. A lower DTI is generally better.
