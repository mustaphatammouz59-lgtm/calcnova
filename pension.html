How Defined Benefit Pensions Work

A defined benefit pension plan promises a specific monthly payment in retirement. The amount is usually calculated using a formula that takes into account your years of service and your average salary during your final years of employment. The "accrual rate" is the percentage of your final salary you earn for each year of service.
